1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Recorder HD ("we", "us", or "the app") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to complying with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA guidelines.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Our app functions primarily as an offline utility tool for capturing device screen activities:

  • Recorded Videos & Media: When you record your screen, the resulting MP4 video files are stored strictly on your local device storage (under Movies/ScreenRecordings/). We have no access to these recordings, and they are never uploaded to any remote server or third party without your manual, explicit sharing action.
  • Microphone Audio: When the microphone audio option is toggled ON, audio from your microphone is mixed directly into the local MP4 video file on-device. Audio is never live-streamed or transferred to external servers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide personal identity details (such as your name, phone number, or physical address) to use the application.

3. Android Permissions & Justifications

To perform screen recording and respect Google Play least-privilege policies, Screen Recorder HD requests the following permissions strictly for their stated functional purposes:

Permission Purpose & Justification
RECORD_AUDIO Optional runtime permission. Used exclusively to capture your voice commentary and surrounding ambient audio during screen recording when enabled by the user.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION Required by Android 14+ system architecture to maintain an active screen-recording session and display persistent control notifications (pause, resume, finish) while other apps are on screen.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing screen recorder notification, chronometer timer, and quick controls.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback to confirm when a recording starts, pauses, or finishes.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to load advertising through Google AdMob and check network connectivity for ad delivery.

5. User Data Control, Export & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over all recordings created with Screen Recorder HD:

  • Exporting: You can export high-definition MP4 files directly to your device's public Gallery or Movies directory at any time using the in-app Export action.
  • Sharing: Recordings can be shared to third-party applications (e.g., WhatsApp, Drive, YouTube) only upon your direct interaction with the system share sheet.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can delete any recording permanently from the app's Videos gallery. Deleting a recording permanently erases the file from your local storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate measures.

7. Security

We value your trust in using Screen Recorder HD. Because your videos never leave your local device, the security of your recordings remains under your direct device protection (such as device PIN, biometric lock, and local encryption). We utilize modern Android scoped storage and official FileProvider mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access by other applications.
